# Video production brief

## 1. Communication outcome
- What must the audience understand, feel or do after watching?
- Who is the primary audience and where will they see it?
- What is the single message the film must retain?
- How will performance be evaluated?

## 2. Deliverables and versions
- Main film duration and aspect ratio
- Cutdowns, teasers and platform-specific versions
- Captioned, clean and textless versions
- Languages, subtitles and voice-over variants
- Thumbnail, poster frame and still-image requirements
- Master and delivery file specifications

## 3. Creative direction
- Format: interview, testimonial, product, animation, event or campaign
- Tone, references and explicit anti-references
- Required claims, proof points and call to action
- Brand assets and mandatory visual elements
- Script, storyboard and treatment responsibility

## 4. Production scope
- Locations, permits and access windows
- On-camera participants and availability
- Casting, styling, props and set requirements
- Crew, equipment and travel assumptions
- Product samples and demonstration requirements
- Weather or contingency plan

## 5. Rights and approvals
- Music, talent, location and stock licensing territory and term
- Release-form responsibility
- Named decision maker
- Legal or compliance review stages
- Consolidated feedback deadline

## 6. Schedule
- Script approval
- Pre-production lock
- Shoot dates
- First cut
- Revision rounds
- Final delivery and campaign launch

## 7. Commercial boundaries
- Budget or working range
- Included shoot days and overtime terms
- Included edit and feedback rounds
- Reshoot triggers and responsibility
- Cancellation and postponement terms
- Archive and project-file retention

## 8. Acceptance checklist
- [ ] Every deliverable and ratio is listed
- [ ] Claims and mandatory wording are approved
- [ ] Rights territory and duration are defined
- [ ] Feedback owner and deadlines are named
- [ ] Technical delivery specifications are confirmed
