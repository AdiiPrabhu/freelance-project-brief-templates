# Social media campaign brief

## 1. Campaign objective
- What business outcome should the campaign influence?
- Which audience segment matters most?
- What action should that audience take?
- Which metric is primary and which are diagnostic only?

## 2. Offer and message
- Product, service or proposition being promoted
- Core audience problem and promise
- Required proof points
- Offer, deadline and eligibility conditions
- Claims that require approval or substantiation
- Call to action and destination URL

## 3. Channel plan
For each channel, define audience, role, format, publishing owner and paid/organic status.

- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- YouTube
- Facebook
- X
- Other communities or creator channels

## 4. Deliverables
- Number of concepts versus resized adaptations
- Static posts, carousels, stories, reels and short video
- Captions, hooks, hashtags and accessibility text
- Landing-page or email dependencies
- Community-management responses
- Editable source-file requirements

## 5. Inputs and approvals
- Brand system and previous performance data
- Product imagery, footage and customer proof
- Named approver and consolidated feedback method
- Legal, platform or brand review requirements
- Creator or partner approval dependencies

## 6. Schedule and publishing
- Creative concept approval
- Production and review dates
- Publishing calendar
- Media launch and optimisation window
- Reporting dates
- Time-zone and response expectations

## 7. Measurement
- Tracking links and analytics owner
- Attribution window
- Paid media budget and management responsibility
- Baseline and target metrics
- Test variables and minimum sample size
- Reporting format and decision cadence

## 8. Commercial boundaries
- Production fee and media budget separated
- Included revision rounds
- New-concept versus adaptation pricing
- Usage rights for talent, creators, music and stock
- Moderation, paid optimisation and reporting exclusions
- Change-request approval process

## 9. Pre-quote decisions
- [ ] Objective and audience prioritised
- [ ] Deliverable matrix approved
- [ ] Media responsibility confirmed
- [ ] Rights and claims reviewed
- [ ] Tracking and reporting access confirmed
